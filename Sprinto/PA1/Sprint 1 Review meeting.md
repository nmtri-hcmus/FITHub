  * Problem: Trello cards assignment.

  * Cause: At first, meeting length was too long as we jumped into meetings without prior establishment of meeting agenda. While this allowed everyone to freely brainstorm and be creative, this also resulted in a long meeting times.

* **What can be done differently in the next sprint to improve:** 
  * Assign Trello tasks during the Planning meeting, or at least right after a Planning meeting.
  * Keep announcements/communication to the point, and not be too wordy/unconcise. Still encourage communication between team members.
  * Establish meeting agendas and goals prior to having them, and prioritize finishing what's on the agenda before moving onto brainstorming/discussions.

* **What lessons we could learn:** 
  * We need to better define and establish/identify most relevant problems/tasks first in order to ensure a smoother and more logical workflow for the team.
